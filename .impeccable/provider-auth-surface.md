# Provider authentication surface

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** One secure threshold separates provider operations from clinical work. It refuses the generic admin-login card floating over decorative healthcare imagery.

**OWN-WORLD:** Roboto, navy, royal blue, white and pale-neutral surfaces, fine cool borders, modest radii, folded-document EPR mark, Lucide line icons, and text-labelled status states continue the implemented Dashboard language.

**STORY:** An unauthenticated workforce user reaches sign-in, can recover from invalid credentials without losing their username, and enters only the portal allowed by their server-returned role. The provider landing view immediately states that global scope contains no patient records and that tenant administration is not connected.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Desktop splits a focused form from an explicitly illustrative workflow field. Tablet stacks that field above the form. Mobile compresses the illustration without hiding the form action. The protected provider shell uses desktop sidebar, tablet rail, and mobile bottom navigation.

**FORM:** Brief-pinned extension of the existing EPR application shell, first choice in the local-extension list. Seed key: `brief-pinned-existing-epr-provider-auth-v1` (no randomized concept round was run for this precisely scoped extension).

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Quality bar

- Login purpose, primary action, validation, loading, network failure, and credential failure are legible without relying on color.
- Provider scope and the no-patient-record boundary are visible before any portal feature summary.
- Unimplemented tenant, support, search, and audit capabilities say “Not connected” and do not resemble available controls.
- The EPR mark, typography, palette, spacing, and responsive navigation remain visibly one system across sign-in and provider shell.
- Keyboard focus, labels, 44 px touch targets, 4.5:1 body-text contrast, session refresh, and sign-out are verified at 1440×1000, 834×1194, and 390×844.
