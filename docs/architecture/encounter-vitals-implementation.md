# Connected synthetic encounter vital signs

The connected note has a **Vital signs** section before template fields. It shows one current card per recorded measurement, ordered by measurement time across this patient's encounters at the authorized Practice site. A measurement from June, yesterday, and today appears as three dated cards. The clinician can add a measurement to the current encounter, edit it, or remove an unsigned entry recorded in error. The UI does not present edit revisions as historical measurements or require a correction-reason field. New entries and edits record the fixed source `Clinic (manual)`.

Tenant V17 stores encounter-linked measurements with immutable internal correction evidence. Tenant V18 adds a removal marker to the measurement head. Removal hides an entry from the clinical timeline and unsigned note without deleting its source revision or audit trail. Optimistic versions and idempotency keys prevent stale edits, removals, and duplicate retries. Current-encounter writes lock the encounter row shared with signing; removal is rejected after the note is signed. Blood pressure requires both values, other fields may remain unrecorded, and times use Philippine time.

The patient timeline endpoint under `/api/v1/practices/{practiceId}/patients/{patientId}/encounters/{encounterId}/vital-sets/timeline` paginates current measurements from the clinician's authorized encounters at the selected site. It checks access to the requested encounter and filters the timeline by Practice, patient, site, and responsible clinician. Existing `GET` and `POST` at `/vital-sets`, `POST /{vitalSetId}/corrections`, and `POST /{vitalSetId}/remove` serve exact encounter reads and writes. Commands require `siteId` and `Idempotency-Key`. Clinical routing requires tenant schema 19 after the draft deletion extension.

Only the current, nonremoved measurements from the active encounter enter its exact note candidate. Signature confirmation re-renders and rejects a candidate made stale by an edit or removal. Previously signed bytes remain immutable if a later correction is recorded; a linked amendment is required to change signed narrative.

Verification: `./api/mvnw -f api/pom.xml verify` runs PostgreSQL integration tests for migration, isolation, dated records across visits, idempotent removal, correction conflicts, candidate staleness, and signed-byte immutability. Frontend lint, tests, build, and three-layout browser verification follow `web/AGENTS.md`. Browser checks with synthetic HTTP responses do not prove backend authorization.

Remaining gates: this slice has no nurse intake role, device integration, validated measurement ranges or alert thresholds, trend graph, or real-patient approval. The clinician-only site and encounter authorization does not claim a complete observation service for other workflows.
